I am a 4th year PhD researcher in moral psychology & AI ethics. I am affiliated with [School of PPLS](https://www.ed.ac.uk/ppls) at the University of Edinburgh, and with [Centre for Technomoral Futures](https://www.technomoralfutures.uk), an interdisciplinary research centre at [Edinburgh Futures Institute](https://efi.ed.ac.uk) focusing on unifying technical and moral knowledge to address ethical implications of AI/data-driven technologies. 

Advised by [Dr Adam Moore](https://www.ed.ac.uk/profile/adam-moore) and [Prof Shannon Vallor](https://www.ed.ac.uk/profile/shannon-vallor), I work at the intersection of psychology and philosophy with a particular focus on the \textbf{moral psychology of AI}, exploring 1) how people’s moral psychology influences their judgements about AI-generated content and AI systems, and 2) how and what should be the way in which AI shapes people’s moral decision-making. I use quantitative (frequentist \& Bayesian) methods in R. My current primary research interests include machine ethics, human-AI interaction, (AI) moral enhancement, moral intuition and heuristics, moral belief updating, moral responsibility/blame/praise.

I received my M.A. Psychology (2020) and MSc.R. Psychology (2021) from the University of Edinburgh. 

Feel free to [email](mailto:yuxin.liu@ed.ac.uk) or dm me on [twitter](https://twitter.com/_yuxinl_/).
